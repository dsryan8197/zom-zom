## Sound (cut, trimmed and normalised to mono MP3 in `public/audio/`)

- **Gunshots:** [The Free Firearm Sound Library](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library)
  (CC0, uploaded to OpenGameArt by bart): real recordings of a Springfield 1917, S&W 642, Carl Gustav M45,
  AR-15, Charles Daly and Benelli Nova shotguns, AK-47, PPSh and Savage Model 10.
- **Zombies:** [Zombie moans](https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-moans) and
  [Hungry ghoul](https://opengameart.org/content/hungry-ghoul) by Darsycho;
  [Zombie noises and moans](https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-noises-and-moans) by ianzazz;
  [Ghost Monster Voice Moaning & Growling](https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-monster-voice-moaning-growling)
  by qubodup (pitched down for juggernauts). All CC0.
- **Explosions:** [Explosion](https://opengameart.org/content/explosion-0) by tinyworlds (CC0), plus
  `explosionCrunch` / `lowFrequency_explosion` from [Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) (CC0).
- **Foley:** footsteps, flesh and bullet impacts, shell casings from
  [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds); reload clicks, latches and swings from
  [Kenney RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio); lasers, zaps and thrusters from Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds. All CC0.
